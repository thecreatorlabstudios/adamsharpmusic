My Story

I’ve always been a musician. I love music.

Adam has been making music for as long as he can remember. He started leading worship in high school, and that calling took him on mission trips with YWAM.

Two things sit at the center of his work. One is multilingual worship, singing to God in more than one language. The other is identity renewal within Christians.

“My heart is that all will come to know Christ as I have known Him, so that they can experience the same love that I have.”Adam Sharp

His audience is broad on purpose. Some people are hearing his music for the first time and some have followed him for years.
